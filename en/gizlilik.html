Privacy Policy

İlaç Takip · Last updated: 24 November 2026

İlaç Takip is a reminder and record-keeping app that reminds you to take your medicines, creams, vitamins and water, and, if you wish, lets you note what you eat and keep a health card. This page explains what information is kept and where. In short: your data stays only on your phone, and we collect none of it.

What information is kept?

  • The name, form, dose, application area, notes and schedule of the medicines, creams and vitamins you add
  • Which dose you took, skipped or postponed and when; notes you write on a dose
  • Stock information you enter for the remaining amount of a medicine
  • Your water log and your water goal
  • Foods and portions you mark in the food diary (Pro)
  • What you write on the health card: height, weight, year of birth, conditions, allergies, blood group, the name and phone number of the person to call in an emergency, notes
  • If you keep a medicine or health card for someone else (for example "My mother"), the name and information you write for that person
  • Your app settings (quiet hours, notification sound, etc.)

Where is it kept?

All of this information is stored only on your own device, in the app's own storage area. The app has no server; your information is not sent to us or to any other company. You do not need to create an account. The app does not write this information to iCloud or to any other cloud service; on iPhone the app's data file is excluded from iCloud backup, and on Android automatic cloud backup is turned off. For this reason, records are not transferred automatically when you change phones.

Health card and records kept for someone else

The health card is optional; you do not have to fill in any field. From height and weight, the app only calculates the body mass index; it draws no conclusion other than what is written on the card, makes no diagnosis and sends this information nowhere.

The card can be seen by anyone who picks up your phone. If you do not want this, turn on the app lock in Settings. The lock uses your phone's own face, fingerprint or passcode verification; the app does not have access to your face or fingerprint data.

If you keep records for someone else (a family member, a person you care for), we recommend that you enter their information with their knowledge and permission. The name and phone number you enter as an emergency contact also stay only on your phone.

Home screen widget

The widget on iPhone shares the information needed to show the next dose and the daily water intake only on the same phone, between the app and the widget. This information also does not leave the device.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; they are not sent over the internet. If you wish, you can use the "Hide medicine name in notification" option in Settings to prevent the medicine name from appearing on the lock screen.

Subscription and payment

The Pro subscription is purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes your payment information; this information does not reach us. The app only asks the store whether your subscription is active. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply.

Advertising, tracking and analytics

The app contains no advertising. No third-party software for tracking, analytics or advertising is used. Your health information is not used for marketing or advertising.

Export, import and PDF report

The "Export data" and "PDF report" features create a file and open your phone's share sheet. You decide where to send the file; the app does not send the file anywhere on its own. The file contains your health information (the export file contains the health cards; a PDF report for a single person also contains that person's card); be careful about who you share it with.

The app occasionally reminds you to "Back up your data". This is only a reminder; nothing leaves your phone unless you create the file and save it somewhere.

The "Import data" feature reads, on your phone, a file that you exported earlier and selected yourself, and replaces the records on the phone with it. The file is not sent anywhere; the app does not access files you have not selected. The Pro subscription is not obtained from the file.

Source links

The links on the "Sources" screen open the web page of the relevant institution in your browser when you tap them. The privacy policies of those sites apply; the app does not send any information about you to those sites.

Retention period and deletion

Your records stay on your phone until you delete them (water and food records older than 400 days are cleaned up automatically). You can delete all your records with Settings > "Delete all data". Removing the app from your phone also deletes all data. Because we do not keep your data, there is no record on our side to delete.

Your rights

Because your information does not reach us, we do not collect, store, process or transfer to anyone any personal data of yours. Viewing, correcting, exporting and deleting your records is entirely in your hands and is done from within the app. Nevertheless, for questions under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, you may write to us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone. A parent may keep records for a child; these records also stay only on the parent's phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.

Summary

İlaç Takip is a reminder and diary tool. It stores all data (medicines, dose history and notes, stock, water log, food diary, the optional health card with height, weight, conditions, allergies, blood group and an emergency contact, records kept for family members, settings) only on your device. There is no server and no account; we collect no data, and the app does not write your data to iCloud or any other cloud service. Reminders are local notifications. The optional app lock uses the phone's own Face ID, fingerprint or passcode; the app never sees biometric data. The export and PDF report features create a file and open the system share sheet; you decide where it goes. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google; we only check whether a subscription is active. No ads, tracking or analytics SDKs.
