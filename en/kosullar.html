Medical disclaimer

İlaç Takip is only a reminder and record-keeping tool; it is not a medical device, gives no medical advice, makes no diagnosis, recommends no treatment, calculates no doses and does not check drug interactions. The dose, frequency and manner of use of your medicines are always determined by your doctor or pharmacist. The nutrition section is only a daily summary based on the foods you mark; the amounts and percentages it shows are estimates calculated from publicly available nutrition tables, are not measurements and contain no assessment of your health. The health card only displays what you have written; the app does not interpret any number, including the body mass index. Your phone's battery-saving and notification settings may delay reminders; do not rely on this app alone for important medicines. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU).
